What's a rendezvous?

Without a rendezvous, One Link can only find devices on the same Wi-Fi network as you. To chat with a laptop at home from a coffee shop — or with friends and family across the country — your devices need a way to find each other across the internet.

A rendezvous is a small server that lets paired devices announce where they currently are. It never sees your messages or files; it only carries signed presence beacons. Add one URL and your devices will find each other anywhere on Earth.

Three options:

  • Use a rendezvous a friend or community is running.
  • Run your own (free, ~5 minutes — see RENDEZVOUS_DEPLOY.md in the repo).
  • Don't add any. Wi-Fi-only mode is fully secure and works for household use.
